The built-in image generation tool received this final prompt for `paper-radar-sculpture.png`:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one vertical editorial image for the lead Work card on a personal blog, to sit behind live HTML type. Create a sophisticated tactile still life representing a personal academic paper radar: a sculptural fan or circular wave of real off-white research paper sheets, their edges and folds sharply lit, arranged in a precise radial motion against a deep near-black studio background. A single tiny vivid orange square or paper tab accents the composition, echoing the site's orange details. Restrained high-contrast art direction, physical material, strong depth, subtle movement implied by arrangement, generous dark negative space in the top and lower third for overlay text, premium experimental editorial photography, portrait 4:5 composition. No visible words or letters, no interface, no screens, no charts, no fake software, no logos, no people, no purple gradients, no glow.

The three Journal card paths are editorial summaries of the linked original essays rather than quotations: writing/routing/returning for `reconnect-blog-content-notes`, query/trace/cite for `narrarc-agentic-rag-architecture-note`, and receive/generate/send for `genericagent-wechat-reliability`.
